# In your own words

Before any test execution, restate the work in your own words. Do not drive the app until these three lines exist.

- **Testing goal.** The behavior this run will prove or disprove.
- **Target surface.** The real UI or CLI a user would touch.
- **Risk hypothesis.** The way that behavior could be wrong, and the observation that would show it.

Use facts from the report. Label a guess as a guess. If one of the three cannot be named, say what is missing and stop.
